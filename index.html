RangeSelector

The usability-first date picker.

Period

Features

  • Two masked fields. Each date is typed as dd/mm/yyyy over a __/__/____ guide. When From is complete, the cursor moves to To.
  • Two ways to use the calendar. Clicking a field opens the calendar for that date. The 📅 button opens the range calendar: From first, then To.
  • Open ranges. You can pick only From or only To. The empty side reads “No limit” and is submitted empty.
  • Meaningful empty state. With no dates, the control says what that means: “All dates”, “This month”, etc.
  • Validation. Non-existent dates or dates outside min/max are marked red and block form submission. If From is after To, they are swapped.
  • Keyboard and accessibility. Fully usable without a mouse, with ARIA roles and labels.

Getting started

Include the files

The CSS and the JS. No dependencies.

Add the control

A div with data-range-selector inside your form. It initializes itself.

Read the dates

The form submits desde (from) and hasta (to) as YYYY-MM-DD. Either one may be empty.

Options

Initial values, limits and the text for when there are no dates. The rest is under API.
